Network Debug

Multiplayer needs a direct WebRTC connection between the two devices. Peers on the same Wi-Fi reach each other by their local addresses and never involve NAT at all, which is why LAN play works. Once one device is on mobile data, the connection has to cross the carrier's NAT, and that usually only works through a TURN relay.

Run this on both devices — the laptop, and the phone on mobile data with Wi-Fi switched off. A VPN, iCloud Private Relay or Cloudflare WARP changes the answer, so turn those off first if you want to measure the network itself.
